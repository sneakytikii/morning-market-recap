The running story has now split in two, and it helps to keep them apart. The first is the one the election started. Tuesday's vote handed the Democrats both houses of Congress and several states where the big data centres are being built, and by Thursday investors were pricing in a slower build-out of the computer halls that artificial intelligence runs on. That argument is about permission rather than orders, and no company can answer it with a good quarter. The second is new, and it belongs to one company. Over the weekend Nvidia's board opened an independent investigation into its chief executive, Jensen Huang, after reports in the New York Times and the Financial Times about his relationship with a Chinese businesswoman whom US intelligence is said to have identified as working for Beijing. He apologised to staff and remains in the job; the board said it has no reason to believe national security or company secrets were given away; China's foreign ministry called the reports baseless. Three House committee chairmen have asked him to brief them privately on November 18 — the day after Nvidia reports.

What to watch, and the two warnings that have not changed. The dates: the October inflation report tomorrow, Tuesday, November 10, at 5:30 in the morning Pacific time — the last inflation reading the Fed sees before it decides on December 9, because November's figure does not arrive until the day after the vote — and then Nvidia's results on Tuesday, November 17, after the close. First warning: four of your five are the same AI-chip bet — Nvidia outright, the largest single piece of both SPX and QQQ, and levered three times inside SOXL. Five weeks running, those four have fallen every week and Costco has risen every week. Second warning: SOXL's daily reset. Last week the chip index fell about 13% and SOXL fell about 40.3% — from $77.05 to $45.97, about $31 a share. Friday ran the same machine the other way, up 12.48%, and gave back about $5.10 of it. Over five weeks the index is down about 30% and the fund about 72%; the index needs about +44% to get back to October 2, the fund about +256%. Nobody can tell you which way any of it resolves.
