Today · Tuesday, September 15오늘 · 9월 15일 화요일

A quieter day than yesterday so far, with one turn worth noticing. Chip shares opened higher this morning, bouncing after yesterday's heavy fall, and have since handed almost all of it back: SOXL was up about 5% at its best and is now roughly where it finished yesterday, while Nvidia has slipped from about 1% up to barely changed. QQQ is down about half a percent and the S&P 500 about the same, with Costco the weakest of your five, down about 1%. The weight on all of it is the same as yesterday — the government's ten-year borrowing rate is around 5%, its highest since 2007, and oil has risen again to near $106 a barrel. The Federal Reserve decides tomorrow at 11 in the morning Pacific time, and a rate rise is widely expected.

어제보다는 조용한 하루이지만, 눈여겨볼 대목이 하나 있습니다. 반도체 주식은 어제의 큰 하락 뒤 반등하며 오늘 아침 높게 출발했다가, 오른 만큼을 거의 다 되돌렸습니다. SOXL은 한때 5%가량 올랐다가 지금은 어제 마친 자리 부근이고, 엔비디아도 1%가량 상승에서 거의 제자리로 내려왔습니다. QQQ는 0.5%가량 내렸고 S&P 500도 비슷하며, 보유 다섯 중 가장 약한 코스트코는 1%가량 내렸습니다. 전체를 누르는 것은 어제와 같습니다. 정부의 10년 만기 차입 금리가 5% 부근으로 2007년 이후 가장 높고, 유가는 다시 올라 배럴당 106달러 부근입니다. 연방준비제도는 내일 서부 시각 오전 11시에 결정합니다. 금리 인상이 널리 예상됩니다.

Monday, in review월요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday the market split in two, and the headline number hid it. The S&P 500 lost only 0.48% — but underneath, the companies that make artificial-intelligence machinery were sold hard while the companies that use it rose. Chip shares as a group fell 5.63%. That took SOXL ≈ −16.98% in a single session, about a sixth of its value, and NVDA ≈ −3.36%. On the very same day Adobe rose 5.30%, Alphabet 3.22% and the security firm CrowdStrike 13.85% — which is why QQQ lost just 0.80% holding both sides at once. COST ≈ +1.56% was the only one of your five to finish higher, and the best of them. What set it off was the weekend: the heads of Anthropic and OpenAI both said publicly that the industry should slow down how fast it makes these programs cleverer.

어제 시장은 둘로 갈라졌고, 겉에 보이는 숫자가 그것을 가렸습니다. S&P 500은 0.48%밖에 잃지 않았습니다. 그러나 그 아래에서는 인공지능 기계를 만드는 회사들이 크게 팔리고, 그것을 쓰는 회사들은 올랐습니다. 반도체 주식은 업종 전체가 5.63% 내렸습니다. 그 결과 SOXL ≈ −16.98%로 하루 만에 가치의 약 6분의 1이 빠졌고, NVDA ≈ −3.36%였습니다. 바로 같은 날 어도비가 5.30%, 알파벳이 3.22%, 보안 회사 크라우드스트라이크가 13.85% 올랐습니다. 양쪽을 함께 담고 있는 QQQ가 0.80%만 내린 이유가 그것입니다. COST ≈ +1.56%는 보유 다섯 중 유일하게 상승으로 마쳤고, 그중 가장 좋았습니다. 이 일을 시작시킨 것은 주말이었습니다. 앤스로픽과 오픈AI의 대표가 업계가 이 프로그램들을 똑똑하게 만드는 속도를 늦춰야 한다고 나란히 공개적으로 말했습니다.

The bigger running story is the cost of borrowing, and yesterday it reached a number people notice. The rate the American government pays to borrow for ten years touched 5.01% during the day — above 5% for the first time since October 2023 — before settling near 4.96%. The thirty-year sits about 5.35% and ordinary home loans crossed 7% last week for the first time in over a year. Oil is what is driving it. Saudi Arabia's main east-west pipeline is still shut after being attacked, American crude closed above $100 a barrel again, and diesel at American pumps set an all-time record of $6.23 a gallon. Diesel is what lorries, trains and farm machinery run on, so it sits inside the price of almost everything else. That is why a fall in chip shares did not simply get bought back, and it is why everything now points at one appointment: the Federal Reserve announces tomorrow, Wednesday September 16, at 11 in the morning Pacific time. A rate rise is widely expected — different measures of the odds run between about 85% and 92%. Nobody can tell you what it will actually do.

더 큰 흐름은 돈을 빌리는 값이고, 어제 그것이 사람들이 알아채는 숫자에 닿았습니다. 미국 정부가 10년 동안 돈을 빌릴 때 무는 금리가 장중에 5.01%까지 닿았습니다. 2023년 10월 이후 처음으로 5%를 넘은 것이고, 이후 4.96% 부근으로 내려 마쳤습니다. 30년물은 약 5.35%이고, 일반 주택담보대출 금리는 지난주에 1년여 만에 처음으로 7%를 넘었습니다. 이것을 밀어 올리는 것은 유가입니다. 사우디아라비아의 주요 동서 송유관은 공격을 받은 뒤 여전히 닫혀 있고, 미국산 원유는 다시 배럴당 100달러 위에서 마쳤으며, 주유소 경유 값은 갤런당 6.23달러로 사상 최고를 기록했습니다. 경유는 화물차와 기차, 농기계가 쓰는 연료여서 거의 모든 물건의 값 안에 들어 있습니다. 반도체 주식의 하락이 그냥 되사들여지지 않은 이유가 그것이고, 이제 모든 것이 한 약속을 가리키는 이유이기도 합니다. 연방준비제도가 내일, 곧 9월 16일 수요일 서부 시각 오전 11시에 발표합니다. 금리 인상이 널리 예상되며, 확률을 재는 자료마다 약 85%에서 92% 사이입니다. 실제로 무엇을 할지는 아무도 말해 줄 수 없습니다.

Two structural things to carry into the rest of this week. The first is that yesterday proved both of the warnings this page keeps repeating, in one session. Four of your five holdings are the same bet — Nvidia outright, inside SPX, inside QQQ, and levered three times in SOXL — and when the chip news is genuinely bad there is nowhere in those four to hide. All four fell together. And SOXL is the one that can hurt you fastest: it fell 16.98% in a day. Look at the five-day figure to see the second cost. Chip shares are down 4.32% over five trading days; three times that is 12.96%; SOXL is down 13.77%. That extra 0.81 of a percentage point is not the fund going wrong — it did exactly what it promises on each of those days, and doing it fresh every day is what cost the difference. The second is that the split itself is new. For two years this market moved as one lump. Yesterday it sold one idea without selling everything. One session does not establish a pattern, and it is worth watching whether it lasts.

Reasons it could go up

The demand is still other companies' accounts, not Nvidia's claims. Oracle — which buys these machines and rents them out — said the work it has signed and not yet delivered now stands at $664 billion, well above the roughly $631 billion expected, and confirmed it will spend $90–95 billion on equipment this year. That spending is the clearest possible statement about future chip orders, and the market believed it enough to send Hewlett Packard Enterprise up 12.2% and Dell up 11.5% on Friday. Dell is sitting on a record $95 billion of unfilled AI server orders, and those servers run on Nvidia chips. Broadcom's AI chip sales rose 221% in a year. Nvidia's own August figures said the same: $96.2 billion of sales in three months, about $108 billion guided for the next three, nothing counted from China. And it keeps buying its way wider rather than waiting — $12.9 billion for Hugging Face last week, on top of August's $3.5 billion into MediaTek. After last week's fall the shares are cheaper against all of that than they were seven days ago.

Reasons it could go down

The weekend added a risk this share cannot argue with: the people who build the leading artificial-intelligence programs said the industry should slow down making them.
