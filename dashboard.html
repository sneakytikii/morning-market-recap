Monday, in review월요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday the market finally caught its breath. Chip shares had fallen eight days running — their longest losing streak in well over a decade — and buyers came back at last: the chip index rose 1.61%, the Nasdaq added 1.11%, and the S&P 500 rose 0.41% to 7,690.73. But the bounce thinned as the day went on. The index faded from its morning high, eight of its eleven industry groups still fell, and the Dow slipped. One up day after eight down ones is a breath, not a recovery.

어제 시장이 마침내 숨을 돌렸습니다. 반도체 주식이 8거래일 내리 하락한 — 십수 년 만에 가장 긴 연속 하락 — 끝에 드디어 매수세가 돌아왔습니다. 반도체 지수가 1.61%, 나스닥이 1.11% 올랐고, S&P 500은 0.41% 오른 7,690.73으로 마쳤습니다. 다만 반등은 갈수록 얇아졌습니다. 지수는 오전 고점에서 밀렸고, 11개 업종 중 8개는 여전히 내렸으며, 다우존스는 소폭 하락했습니다. 여드레 내린 뒤의 하루 상승은 회복이 아니라 숨 고르기입니다.

What knocked the market off its record was a change of question. Two weeks ago the S&P 500 closed at a record above 7,800. Then investors began asking how the AI build-out is being paid for. OpenAI told investors its revenue was about $20 billion smaller than had been widely reported; companies are borrowing heavily to buy chips and build data centres; and a study from the bank that serves the world's central banks found much of the money moves in a circle — chipmakers helping fund the very customers who buy their chips. A hot September inflation number — 3.7%, pushed up mostly by gasoline — landed in the middle of that doubt and revived talk of another rate rise. Chip shares lost roughly $1 trillion of value in a week. The money did not leave the market; it moved — into bonds, into steadier companies like the ones in the Dow, and into Costco, up about 9.4% this month and knocking on $1,000.

시장을 기록에서 끌어내린 것은 질문이 바뀐 일입니다. 2주 전 S&P 500은 7,800선 위의 기록 종가를 썼습니다. 그 뒤 투자자들이 AI 건설 비용을 누가 어떻게 대고 있는지 묻기 시작했습니다. OpenAI는 매출이 널리 알려진 것보다 약 200억 달러 적다고 투자자들에게 밝혔고, 기업들은 반도체를 사고 데이터센터를 짓기 위해 돈을 크게 빌리고 있으며, 세계 중앙은행들의 은행이 낸 연구는 그 돈의 상당 부분이 원을 그리며 돈다고 — 반도체 회사가 자기 반도체를 사 줄 고객의 자금을 돕는다고 — 짚었습니다. 그 의심 한가운데에 뜨거운 9월 물가 — 주로 휘발유가 밀어 올린 3.7% — 가 떨어지며 금리 추가 인상 이야기를 되살렸습니다. 반도체 주식은 한 주 동안 대략 1조 달러의 가치를 잃었습니다. 돈은 시장을 떠난 게 아니라 옮겨 갔습니다 — 채권으로, 다우존스에 든 것 같은 더 안정적인 회사들로, 그리고 이번 달 약 9.4% 올라 1,000달러 문을 두드리는 코스트코로요.

From here the test is receipts. This week about 70 of America's biggest companies report earnings — Netflix and the rocket company SpaceX tonight, Tesla and Meta tomorrow — and the Federal Reserve decides on rates next Wednesday. The standing warnings are louder than usual: four of your five are the same chip bet, and SOXL triples whatever chips do each day — these two weeks that cost about 40%, and it now needs roughly +206% to see its old high again. Nobody can tell you whether yesterday was the turn or a pause.

이제부터의 시험은 영수증입니다. 이번 주에 미국의 가장 큰 회사 약 70곳이 실적을 내놓습니다 — 오늘 밤 Netflix와 로켓 회사 SpaceX, 내일 밤 테슬라와 메타 — 그리고 다음 주 수요일에는 연방준비제도가 금리를 결정합니다. 늘 붙는 경고는 평소보다 더 큽니다. 보유 다섯 중 넷은 같은 반도체 베팅이고, SOXL은 반도체가 하는 일의 세 배를 매일 합니다 — 지난 2주 동안 그것이 약 40%를 가져갔고, 이제 옛 고점을 다시 보려면 대략 +206%가 필요합니다. 어제가 전환점인지 잠시 멈춤인지는 아무도 말해 줄 수 없습니다.

Reasons it could go up

Even the worst week since June cost the index only about 1.9%, and it sits within about 1.6% of its record — the money that fled the chip names did not leave, it bought the rest of the index: the Dow rose last week, and Costco and the staples set the pace. Rates have become a quiet help — the ten-year is near 5.14%, well below its 5.33% peak — and gasoline-driven inflation is the kind officials have often said they look through. TSMC's record quarter and raised outlook say the AI demand itself is still real, whatever the financing questions. This week, about 70 companies get the chance to answer doubt with numbers.

Reasons it could go down

The question that broke the rally is not answered, only paused. OpenAI's revenue is about $20 billion smaller than was reported, the build-out is increasingly financed with debt, and a study from the central banks' bank found the money often moves in a circle — chipmakers funding their own customers. Hot inflation — 3.7%, with the core rising to 2.6% — has put a Fed rise back on the table for this year even if not this month, and oil above $100 keeps feeding it. Monday's bounce was a handful of names while eight of eleven industry groups fell. If this week's earnings disappoint — or merely confirm the borrowing — the week just repaired can repeat.
