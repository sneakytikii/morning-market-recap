Today · Thursday, August 27오늘 · 8월 27일 목요일

The morning is shaping up around one report. Nvidia beat expectations after last night's close and is indicated about 7% higher before the open, which is pulling the whole technology side up with it — Nasdaq futures point up around 1% while Dow futures sit near flat, so this is a technology morning rather than a whole-market one. Two other big software names reported the same evening and are also sharply higher: Salesforce and CrowdStrike. Going the other way, HP is down about 10% on its own results. Today's calendar is light by comparison: jobless claims at 5:30 this morning Pacific time, Best Buy before the open and Marvell — another chip company — after the close. The Jackson Hole conference opens today, but the speech that matters is the new Fed chairman's, and that is tomorrow morning. One thing worth knowing before you look: a jump before the opening bell is not the same as a good day, and Nvidia's shares have fallen after six of their last eight reports.

오늘 아침은 실적 발표 하나를 중심으로 짜이고 있습니다. 엔비디아가 어젯밤 장 마감 뒤 예상을 넘겼고 개장 전에 약 7% 높게 지시되면서 기술주 전체를 끌어올리고 있습니다. 나스닥 선물은 1% 안팎 위를 가리키는 반면 다우 선물은 제자리 근처여서, 시장 전체가 아니라 기술주의 아침입니다. 같은 저녁 실적을 낸 다른 대형 소프트웨어 두 곳도 크게 올라 있습니다. 세일즈포스와 크라우드스트라이크입니다. 반대로 HP는 자체 실적에 약 10% 내려 있습니다. 오늘 일정은 그에 비하면 가볍습니다. 서부 시각 오늘 아침 5시 30분에 실업수당 청구 건수, 개장 전에 베스트바이, 장 마감 뒤에 또 다른 반도체 회사 마벨이 있습니다. 잭슨홀 회의는 오늘 시작하지만 중요한 연설은 새 연준 의장의 것이고, 그것은 내일 아침입니다. 보시기 전에 알아 두실 것 하나: 개장 전의 급등이 곧 좋은 하루인 것은 아니며, 엔비디아 주가는 최근 여덟 번의 발표 중 여섯 번은 그 뒤 내렸습니다.

Wednesday, in review수요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday almost nothing happened, and then the biggest thing of the month happened after everyone had gone home. The board barely moved all day — the S&P 500 closed at 7,675.70, down 0.02%, which is a fall of a point and a half; QQQ +0.09%, SOXL +0.80%, COST −0.41%. The one that moved was Nvidia, down 1.59% to $209.66 — nerves in the hours before it had to report, while the rest of the chip shares edged higher. Then, after the close, it reported.

어제는 거의 아무 일도 없었고, 그러다 모두가 퇴근한 뒤에 이달 가장 큰 일이 일어났습니다. 표의 숫자는 하루 종일 거의 움직이지 않았습니다. S&P 500은 7,675.70으로 0.02% 내렸는데 1.5포인트가량 떨어진 것이고, QQQ +0.09%, SOXL +0.80%, COST −0.41%였습니다. 움직인 하나는 엔비디아로 1.59% 내려 209.66달러가 됐습니다. 실적을 내야 하는 몇 시간 전의 긴장이었고, 나머지 반도체 주식들은 조금씩 올랐습니다. 그리고 장이 닫힌 뒤, 발표가 나왔습니다.

The numbers beat, and not narrowly. Nvidia sold $96.2 billion of chips in three months — about $4 billion more than expected and more than double a year ago — earned $2.22 a share, and told investors to expect about $108 billion in the next three months, a figure that assumes no sales at all into China. Its shares rose about 4.7% in after-hours trading. One line went the other way, and it is the one this page has been waiting on since Monday of last week: the company expects to keep about 74 cents of profit on each sales dollar next quarter instead of 75, because the memory inside its servers costs more. So the cost worry that started this month's selling is real — it is just small so far. Watching whether it stays small is the useful thing to do from here.

숫자는 예상을 넘었고, 아슬아슬하게가 아니었습니다. 엔비디아는 석 달 동안 반도체를 962억 달러어치 팔았습니다. 예상보다 약 40억 달러 많고 1년 전의 두 배가 넘습니다. 주당 2.22달러를 벌었고, 다음 석 달은 약 1,080억 달러를 예상하라고 말했는데, 중국 쪽 매출은 전혀 없다고 가정한 숫자입니다. 주가는 시간외 거래에서 약 4.7% 올랐습니다. 반대 방향으로 간 줄이 하나 있고, 이 페이지가 지난주 월요일부터 기다려 온 것이 바로 그것입니다. 회사는 다음 분기에 매출 1달러당 남기는 이익이 75센트가 아니라 약 74센트가 될 것으로 봅니다. 서버 안의 메모리 값이 올랐기 때문입니다. 그러니 이달의 매도를 시작하게 한 비용 걱정은 실제입니다. 다만 아직은 작습니다. 그것이 계속 작게 머무는지 지켜보는 것이, 여기서부터 할 수 있는 쓸모 있는 일입니다.

Two structural things, as always. The four-way overlap is about to run upward again: one company's results are why the chip shares, the Nasdaq fund and the index are all indicated higher this morning, and the one holding with no chips in it is not. And SOXL's daily reset had its cleanest day of the month — the chip fund rose 0.26%, three times that is 0.78%, and SOXL rose 0.80%, two hundredths of a point above a perfect tripling, after coming six-tenths short on Tuesday. Do not let the good tracking distract from the arithmetic: across three days this week the chip fund is down about 0.9% and SOXL is down about 3.3% — nearly four times the fall, not three. Elsewhere, inflation came in exactly as expected and did not fall, and long-term rates ticked back up. The dated tests left: jobless claims this morning, Marvell's results after the close tonight, and the new Fed chair's first Jackson Hole speech tomorrow at 7 in the morning Pacific time.

Reasons it could go up

The single largest thing hanging over this index has now happened, and it went well: Nvidia sold $96 billion of chips in three months, twice what it sold a year ago, and told investors to expect about $108 billion in the next three. The index walked into that with the fear gauge at 15.21 — calm, not braced — and it is within about 1.6% of its record. The pressure that did the damage all last month has come off its worst: the 30-year rate is near 5.19%, below the 19-year high it hit last week, and the Treasury's bigger bond buying only begins September 9. Inflation is stubborn but it is not accelerating — the Fed's preferred gauge has been flat at 3.3% for two months. And the business economy underneath keeps testing well: the monthly survey of American firms was the strongest since April 2022, and jobless claims fell to 206,000.

Reasons it could go down

One good quarter from one company does not fix two things. The first is rates: a 30-year government bond still pays over 5%, which competes with every expensive share in this index, and Wednesday's inflation report gave the Fed no reason to cut — the gauge it watches has sat at 3.3% for two months against a 2% target. Long-term rates ticked back up on the news, ending two days of relief. The second is the American household, where the news keeps getting worse rather than better: consumer confidence at its lowest since January, new home sales at their weakest since January, and Walmart and Dick's Sporting Goods both marked down hard within six days for saying demand slowed. Add the trade fight, which is now scheduled rather than threatened — more than 700 American products taxed by Canada from September 8, on top of a threat to raise tariffs on all Canadian cars, trucks, parts and steel to 50% in January.
