Reasons it could go up

The selling stopped the moment it got reasons to — two rebound days in a row, both led by exactly the shares that fell hardest, which is what a repricing looks like, not a flight. The money behind the AI build-out keeps showing up with names attached: a $500 billion financing round two weeks ago, Anthropic's share-sale filing on Monday, $63 billion of bank financing on Broadcom's chips on Tuesday.
