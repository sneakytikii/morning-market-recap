Today · Thursday, August 13오늘 · 8월 13일 목요일

The morning has gone well. Wholesale prices for July came in flat — no rise at all, where a small rise had been expected — and the S&P 500 has traded above 7,800 for the first time, up about half a percent so far. Chip shares are doing the lifting, and it is the memory makers leading rather than Nvidia: SOXL is up about 7% and QQQ about 1%, while Nvidia itself is up less than 1% and Costco about 0.4%. Cisco is down about 9% after last night's record results — the same lesson as yesterday, that news this good had already been paid for. Applied Materials reports after tonight's close, and retail sales arrive tomorrow morning at 5:30 Pacific time.

오전 장은 좋게 흘러가고 있습니다. 7월 도매물가가 보합으로 나와 소폭 상승 예상과 달리 전혀 오르지 않았고, S&P 500은 사상 처음으로 7,800을 넘어 지금까지 약 0.5% 오른 상태입니다. 끌어올리는 것은 반도체인데, 앞장선 쪽은 엔비디아가 아니라 메모리 회사들입니다. Wednesday, in review수요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday everything you own went up. The S&P 500 rose 0.26% to 7,748.50, which puts it about a tenth of a percent below its record, and the Nasdaq rose 0.54%. Your five finished: SOXL up 6.89%, NVDA up 3.03%, QQQ up 0.73%, Costco up 0.56%. Only the Dow dissented, slipping 21.58 points — a reminder that this was technology carrying a market that was otherwise standing still.

어제는 보유하신 것이 모두 올랐습니다. S&P 500은 0.26% 올라 7,748.50이 됐는데 사상 최고치보다 0.1% 남짓 낮은 자리이고, 나스닥은 0.54% 올랐습니다. 다섯 종목의 마감은 SOXL +6.89%, NVDA +3.03%, QQQ +0.73%, 코스트코 +0.56%였습니다. 홀로 갈린 것은 다우지수로 21.58포인트 내렸습니다. 이날의 상승이, 나머지는 제자리인 시장을 기술주가 끌고 간 결과였다는 뜻입니다.

Two things did it, and the second one answers a question this page has been carrying for two weeks. The July inflation report came out before the open and was mild — prices up 0.1% over the month and 3.4% over the year, down from June's 3.5%. Then the companies that buy artificial-intelligence equipment reported, and their numbers were large. Super Micro, which builds servers around Nvidia's chips, told investors it expects sales of $65 billion to $72 billion next financial year, well above what analysts had assumed, and rose about 19%. CoreWeave rose about 19% too and Nebius about 34%. Set that against Monday, when Nvidia announced it was raising $500 billion to help its own customers pay for its machines and the shares fell 2.86%. The worry then was that the money was going in a circle. Wednesday was three separate customers reporting that the money is real and that they expect to spend more of it. That is not proof — Nvidia part-owns CoreWeave — but it is a better kind of evidence than a financing agreement, and it lifted the chip group about 2.3%.

두 가지가 그렇게 만들었고, 그중 두 번째는 이 페이지가 2주 동안 안고 있던 질문에 답을 줍니다. 개장 전에 나온 7월 물가 보고서가 순했습니다. 물가는 한 달 사이 0.1%, 1년 사이 3.4% 올라 6월의 3.5%보다 낮았습니다. 이어서 인공지능 장비를 사들이는 회사들이 실적을 내놓았는데 숫자가 컸습니다. 엔비디아 반도체로 서버를 만드는 슈퍼마이크로는 다음 회계연도 매출을 650억~720억 달러로 본다고 밝혔습니다. 분석가들이 예상하던 것보다 훨씬 큰 숫자였고 주가는 약 19% 올랐습니다. 코어위브도 약 19%, 네비우스는 약 34% 올랐습니다. 이것을 월요일과 나란히 놓아 보십시오. 그날 엔비디아는 자기 고객들이 자기 장비를 살 수 있도록 5,000억 달러를 모으겠다고 발표했고 주가는 2.86% 내렸습니다. 그때의 걱정은 돈이 한 바퀴 돌고 있다는 것이었습니다. 수요일은 서로 다른 고객 세 곳이 그 돈이 실제이며 앞으로 더 쓸 것으로 본다고 보고한 날이었습니다. 증명은 아닙니다. 코어위브의 지분은 엔비디아가 갖고 있습니다. 그래도 자금 조달 계약보다는 나은 종류의 근거이고, 반도체 업종을 약 2.3% 끌어올렸습니다.

The structural thing to take from yesterday is what happened after the close. Cisco, which sits inside both QQQ and the S&P 500, reported the best quarter in its history — sales of $17.3 billion, up 18%, and $9.3 billion of AI equipment orders across the year, about four and a half times the year before — and the shares fell about 6%. Nothing was wrong with the numbers. The shares had risen roughly 60% over the year on the expectation of exactly those numbers, so the good news had already been paid for. Keep that in mind for Nvidia on August 26, because Nvidia is up about 11.6% this month heading into its own report and is standing in the same place. The other counterweight has no date on it at all: oil rose for a sixth session running, Brent above $89 a barrel, after a US Navy helicopter disabled a cargo ship running the blockade of Iranian ports — and the 30-year government borrowing rate went up to 5.25% even on the day the inflation figure landed softly. Applied Materials reports after the close tonight.

어제에서 구조적으로 가져가실 것은 장이 끝난 뒤에 일어난 일입니다. QQQ와 S&P 500에 모두 들어 있는 시스코가 회사 역사상 최고 분기를 발표했습니다. 매출 173억 달러로 18% 증가, 그리고 한 해 동안의 AI 장비 수주가 93억 달러로 1년 전의 네 배 반쯤이었습니다. 그런데 주가는 약 6% 내렸습니다. 숫자에 잘못된 것은 없었습니다. 바로 그 숫자를 기대하며 주가가 1년 동안 60%가량 올라 있었으니, 좋은 소식의 값이 이미 치러져 있었던 것입니다. 8월 26일 엔비디아를 생각하실 때 이 점을 기억해 두십시오. 엔비디아도 이번 달에만 약 11.6% 오른 채 자기 실적 발표를 향해 가고 있어 같은 자리에 서 있습니다. 또 하나의 반대 힘에는 아예 날짜가 없습니다. 미 해군 헬기가 이란 항구 봉쇄를 뚫으려던 화물선을 무력화한 뒤 유가가 엿새 연속 올라 브렌트유가 배럴당 89달러를 넘었고, 물가 수치가 부드럽게 나온 날인데도 30년물 국채 금리는 5.25%로 올랐습니다. 오늘 밤 장 마감 후에는 어플라이드 머티어리얼즈가 실적을 발표합니다.

Reasons it could go up

The month's biggest scheduled risk — the July inflation report — came and went without damage, and the next Federal Reserve meeting is not until September 15–16. Company profits are coming in strong, and Wednesday supplied the piece that was missing: the customers buying AI equipment reported real revenue, not promises. The fear gauge fell again, to 14.55, which means investors are relaxed rather than braced. The index is back within about a tenth of a percent of its record and is up about 3.5% for August.

Reasons it could go down

Being back at a record means a lot of good news is already in the price, and Wednesday showed how narrow the support is: the Dow fell on the same day the index rose, so this was technology carrying everything else. Long-term government bonds still pay about 5.25% with far less risk, and that rate went up on Wednesday. Oil has now risen six sessions in a row, with Brent above $89 — the one thing most likely to undo the kind inflation reading the market just celebrated. And last night Cisco reported record sales and record AI orders and its shares fell about 6% regardless. When good news stops moving prices up, the price is doing the talking.

이번 달 예정된 위험 중 가장 큰 7월 물가 보고서가 별 탈 없이 지나갔고, 다음 연방준비제도 회의는 9월 15~16일이나 되어야 있습니다. 회사들의 실적은 좋게 나오고 있고, 수요일에는 그동안 빠져 있던 조각이 채워졌습니다. AI 장비를 사들이는 고객 회사들이 약속이 아니라 실제 매출을 보고했다는 것입니다. 공포 지수는 14.55로 또 내려, 투자자들이 긴장하기보다 편안한 상태임을 보여 줍니다. 지수는 사상 최고치에서 0.1% 남짓 떨어진 자리로 돌아왔고, 8월 누적으로 약 3.5% 상승입니다.

    Signed on Wednesday as a National Security Presidential Memorandum. It sets up a federal programme under which private American companies, once vetted and under contract, may carry out what the memorandum calls “cyber surveillance operations” and “cyber effects operations” — defined to include the manipulation, disruption, denial, degradation or destruction of computer systems — against foreign criminal organisations the government has approved as targets. The National Coordination Center is to establish and run the programme, with the Justice Department and the Department of Homeland Security co-managing it. Officials must give written approval for every operation before a company acts; each participating company must hold a bond or escrow of “not less than $1 million”; and operations that could cause death or serious injury, or that would amount to a use of force under international law, are set aside from the ordinary approval route. No company is named, and no direct quote from him appears in either the memorandum or the fact sheet.
