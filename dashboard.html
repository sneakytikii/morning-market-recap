The week, in review지난 한 주 돌아보기

The market had its best week since August, and it had it in the week Nvidia lost its chief executive. Over the five sessions the S&P 500 rose 2.5% to 7,561.67, ending four straight losing weeks; the Nasdaq rose 2.27% and the Dow 2.07%. Nvidia itself fell 12.7%. That is the week in two numbers: the index went up while its largest company went down. The money did not come into the market, it moved across it — out of the artificial-intelligence shares and into everything else. Among your five, Costco closed Friday at a record and SOXL rose about 27% off a very low base, while NVDA had its sixth losing week in a row.

시장은 8월 이후 가장 좋은 한 주를 보냈고, 그것도 엔비디아가 최고경영자를 잃은 주에 그랬습니다. 다섯 거래일 동안 S&P 500은 2.5% 올라 7,561.67이 되면서 네 주 연속 하락을 끊었고, 나스닥은 2.27%, 다우는 2.07% 올랐습니다. 엔비디아 자신은 12.7% 내렸습니다. 두 숫자로 이번 주가 설명됩니다. 가장 큰 회사가 내리는 동안 지수는 올랐습니다. 돈이 시장으로 새로 들어온 것이 아니라 시장 안에서 옮겨 간 것입니다. 인공지능 주식에서 나와 그 밖의 모든 것으로 갔습니다. 보유 다섯 가운데 Costco는 금요일을 사상 최고 종가로 마쳤고, SOXL은 아주 낮아진 바닥에서 약 27% 올랐으며, NVDA는 여섯 주 연속 하락이었습니다.

Two things sit underneath that, and only one of them eased. The first is Nvidia. Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive on Thursday, November 12, effective at once, and the finance chief, Colette Kress, is standing in while the board looks for a permanent replacement. The shares touched $148.80 that day, the lowest in a year, and on Friday HSBC cut them to hold and lowered its target price from $365 to $170. Across six losing weeks they are down more than 30%, and the company is worth roughly $2 trillion less than at its peak. The second is the interest rate, and it went the wrong way all week: the ten-year government rate ended at about 5.15%, up about 0.29 of a point, and the two-year at about 4.85% after what reporting calls its biggest weekly rise since 2024. Friday’s factory-gate report was cooler than forecast for the month at 0.3%, but its yearly rate of 6.5% is the highest in four years. The one that did ease is the fear gauge, down from 25.64 to 19.30.

What this leaves to watch. Next week is dated and dense: October retail sales on Tuesday, November 17, the first real read on whether the shopper is still spending, with Nvidia’s own results after the close the same day, presented by an interim chief executive; the Federal Reserve’s October minutes on Wednesday, November 18; and the President meeting Xi Jinping at the APEC summit on Thursday, November 19, where chip export rules are the live question. Then December 9, when a rate rise is close to fully priced and the argument is about its size. The two standing risks have not moved. Four of your five holdings are the same bet — Nvidia outright, inside SPX, inside QQQ, and tripled inside SOXL — so a week in which the index rose while Nvidia fell is the exception and not the rule, and one week is not a new pattern. And the levered fund keeps showing why it is the dangerous one: across November the chip index is down about 5% while SOXL is down about 24%, because a fund that resets three times every single day loses ground on a round trip.
