Today · Friday, November 13오늘 · 11월 13일 금요일

Futures point slightly higher this morning — the S&P 500, the Nasdaq and the Dow are each up a fraction — after a session that took a large shock and absorbed it. Nvidia is up around 2% before the bell, carrying on the recovery it began after opening 13% down on Jensen Huang’s resignation. AMD and Broadcom, which rose more than 5% on Thursday, are each a little over 1% lower before the open, so some of the bet on Nvidia’s rivals is being unwound. Cisco is about 2.5% lower even though it beat forecasts and called the quarter a record, while Applied Materials, which also beat and guided higher, is slightly higher. The one number on today’s calendar is factory-gate prices, at 5:30 in the morning Pacific time — what producers charge before goods reach a shop, and a second look at the inflation question behind the December rate decision. Forecasters expect a rise of about 0.4% for October, down from 1.3% the month before. Watch the bond market rather than the share prices: the ten-year government rate jumped to about 5.10% on Thursday, in its first day of trading since Tuesday.

Thursday, in review목요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday the most valuable company in the American market lost its chief executive, and the market barely moved. Nvidia said on Thursday morning that Jensen Huang has resigned, effective at once, after reports of affairs including one with a woman American intelligence is said to have identified as working for Beijing. The finance chief, Colette Kress, is interim chief executive while the board looks for a permanent one, and Harvey Jones becomes the company’s first chairman. The shares opened about 13% lower and touched $148.80, the lowest they have traded in a year — and then won almost all of it back, closing down 4.34% at $166.19. The S&P 500 was down nearly 1% in the first hour and finished down 0.35% at 7,592.40.

어제 미국 시장에서 가장 값비싼 회사가 최고경영자를 잃었는데, 시장은 거의 움직이지 않았습니다. 엔비디아는 목요일 아침 젠슨 황이 사임했다고 밝혔습니다. 즉시 효력이 있고, 베이징을 위해 일한 것으로 미국 정보기관이 판단했다고 보도된 여성을 포함해 여러 관계가 보도된 뒤입니다. 이사회가 정식 후임자를 찾는 동안 재무 책임자 콜레트 크레스가 임시 최고경영자를 맡고, 하비 존스가 이 회사의 첫 이사회 의장이 됩니다. 주가는 약 13% 낮게 출발해 148.80달러까지 내렸습니다. 1년 사이 가장 낮은 값입니다 — 그리고 그 대부분을 되찾아 4.34% 하락한 166.19달러로 마쳤습니다. S&P 500은 첫 한 시간 동안 1% 가까이 내렸다가 0.35% 하락한 7,592.40으로 마감했습니다.

The calm on the surface hid a weak day underneath. Only 123 of the 500 companies rose and 377 fell. Energy was the only one of the eleven parts of the market to finish higher. The index of smaller American companies fell 1.66%. The reason was the bond market, which reopened on Thursday after being shut on Wednesday for Veterans Day and spent the day catching up with Tuesday’s hot inflation report: the ten-year government rate jumped about a tenth of a point to about 5.10%, and the two-year to about 4.86%. Borrowing money got dearer, and smaller firms feel that first. Oil rose as well — American crude settled about 2.6% higher at about $88.74 a barrel, after the President said he is no longer seeking a deal with Iran and after reports of attacks on a Saudi pipeline station. Gold fell about 2.9%.

What this leaves to watch. Nvidia’s question has changed shape rather than gone away. The man has gone, the chair is empty, and the company reports its own results on Tuesday, November 17 with an interim chief presenting them. Three House committees have summoned Huang for a closed-door interview on December 1, and a senator has asked him to appear at a public hearing on December 3. Meanwhile both sets of results that landed after Thursday’s close — Cisco and Applied Materials — beat forecasts, and both shares are flat or lower before this morning’s bell. That is the plainest sign of what is actually setting prices just now: not company results, but the interest rate, and this week it moved against shares.
