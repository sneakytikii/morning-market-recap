Today · Wednesday, August 19오늘 · 8월 19일 수요일

The market opened higher this morning, and the help came from the bond market: before the open the Treasury said it will double its buying-back of its own longest-term debt, and the 30-year rate — which touched a fresh 19-year high during yesterday's session — fell back toward 5.2%. As of about 8:30 this morning Pacific time the whole market is up about half a percent, and the store chains are leading it: Target beat expectations and raised its outlook for the year, its shares up about 5%, and Costco is up about 1.3% alongside. The sore spot is still chips: SOXL is down again, around 5%, after yesterday's 14.80% fall, while Nvidia and QQQ sit near flat. Still ahead today: the Federal Reserve publishes its July meeting minutes at 11 this morning Pacific time — three officials voted to raise rates at that meeting. Walmart reports tomorrow before the open.

Tuesday, in review화요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday the market's favourite trade was the one that got sold. The AI shares that had carried the last three sessions fell hard: Micron dropped 7.02%, one day after its first-ever close above $1,000, SanDisk fell 9.01%, and the gauge of chip shares had its worst day in over two weeks, down 4.96%. The S&P 500 fell 0.69% to 7,691.76, a third straight step back from Thursday's record. Your five split along the chip line again — but the other way this time: Costco rose 0.82%, the only riser, while NVDA fell 2.34%, QQQ fell 1.69%, and SOXL, which triples chip moves, fell 14.80%.

어제는 시장이 가장 아끼던 거래가 팔린 날이었습니다. 지난 사흘을 끌던 AI 주식들이 세게 내렸습니다. 마이크론이 사상 첫 1,000달러 위 마감 하루 만에 7.02% 내렸고, 샌디스크는 9.01% 내렸으며, 반도체 주가지표는 4.96% 내려 2주여 만에 최악의 하루였습니다. S&P 500은 0.69% 내린 7,691.76으로 목요일 사상 최고치에서 세 걸음째 물러섰습니다. 보유 다섯 종목은 이번에도 반도체라는 한 줄을 따라 갈렸는데, 이번에는 반대 방향이었습니다. 코스트코가 0.82% 올라 유일한 상승이었고, NVDA는 2.34%, QQQ는 1.69% 내렸으며, 반도체 움직임을 세 배로 옮기는 SOXL은 14.80% 내렸습니다.

What set it off was not a piece of bad chip news — there was none — but the price of money. During the day the 30-year government rate climbed past 5.33%, a fresh 19-year high, with oil up a third straight day near $91 a barrel and the Strait of Hormuz still shut. And the rate story has grown a new branch that points back at the AI names themselves: the buildout of data centres is being financed with so much borrowed money that reports now count it among the things pushing long-term rates up. Monday's Nvidia pledge — up to $105 billion behind OpenAI's Ohio data centre — is that story in a single number. When shares priced for perfection meet borrowing costs at 19-year highs, the most crowded trade gets repriced first; that is what yesterday was.

방아쇠는 반도체의 나쁜 소식이 아니었습니다. 그런 소식은 없었습니다. 돈의 값이었습니다. 장중에 30년물 국채 금리가 5.33%를 넘어 19년 만의 최고를 새로 썼고, 유가는 사흘 연속 올라 배럴당 91달러 부근이었으며, 호르무즈 해협은 여전히 닫혀 있습니다. 그리고 금리 이야기에는 AI 종목들 자신을 되가리키는 새 가지가 자랐습니다. 데이터센터 구축이 워낙 많은 빚으로 이뤄지다 보니, 보도들이 이제 그것을 장기 금리를 밀어 올리는 요인 중 하나로 꼽는 것입니다. 월요일 엔비디아의 약속, 곧 OpenAI의 오하이오 데이터센터에 최대 1,050억 달러를 보증하겠다는 것은 그 이야기를 숫자 하나로 보여 줍니다. 완벽을 가정한 가격의 주식이 19년 만에 가장 비싼 돈과 만나면, 가장 붐비는 거래부터 값이 다시 매겨집니다. 어제가 바로 그것이었습니다.

Reasons it could go up

Three down days have cost the index all of 1.4% from its record — this is a re-pricing of one crowded trade, not a flight from everything, and the Dow barely fell. The fear gauge, at about 15.8, is nowhere near the levels that mark real alarm. The hard evidence on the shopper keeps improving: Home Depot beat on Tuesday, and Target beat this morning with more customers walking in, and raised its outlook. Wholesale inflation did not rise at all in July, and traders still put the odds of a September rate rise at only about one in three. And the pressure that did the damage got its first real answer this morning: the Treasury stepped in to support the market for its own longest-term debt, and the 30-year rate fell back from its 19-year high.

Reasons it could go down

The market's leadership is the problem: the same AI names that pulled the index to its record are heavy enough to pull it back down, and on Tuesday they did — for a reason with no quick fix. Long-term borrowing costs sit near 19-year highs, oil is near $91 with the Strait of Hormuz still effectively shut, and reports now point at the AI buildout's own enormous borrowing as one of the things pushing long rates up — the boom straining the very rates that value it. A Treasury buyback is relief, not a cure; the deficit and the oil story remain. The shopper test is not finished — Walmart, the biggest, reports tomorrow. And the biggest single test of the crowded trade has a date: Nvidia, a week from today, walking in right after the market showed how it treats AI shares it decides are expensive.
