In plain English쉽게 말하면

Today · Friday, August 28오늘 · 8월 28일 금요일

The new Fed chairman, Kevin Warsh, gave his first big speech this morning — at 7 Pacific time — and said getting inflation down is the job that comes first. Stocks wobbled on that, but only a little: the broad market is down about a third of a percent at midday. The bigger move is in chips, which are handing back part of yesterday's jump — Nvidia is down about 4%, and the chip fund SOXL is down about 10%, with Marvell falling about 7% even though its own results beat expectations. QQQ is down a little under 1%, while Costco is up a little over 1%, its first rise after three down days. The next big number on the calendar is the monthly jobs report, a week from today.

새 연준 의장 케빈 워시가 오늘 아침 서부 시각 7시에 취임 뒤 첫 큰 연설을 했고, 물가를 끌어내리는 일이 가장 먼저라고 말했습니다. 주가는 그 말에 흔들렸지만 폭은 작아서, 한낮 현재 시장 전체는 0.3% 정도 내려 있습니다. 더 큰 움직임은 반도체 쪽입니다. 어제의 급등을 일부 되돌려 주는 중이라 엔비디아는 약 4% 내렸고, 반도체 펀드 SOXL은 약 10% 내렸습니다. 마벨은 예상을 넘긴 실적을 내고도 약 7% 내렸습니다. QQQ는 1%에 조금 못 미치게 내렸고, 코스트코는 1% 남짓 올라 사흘 연속 하락 뒤 처음으로 오르고 있습니다. 달력 위의 다음 큰 숫자는 일주일 뒤에 나오는 월간 고용 보고서입니다.

Thursday, in review목요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday almost nothing happened, and then the biggest thing of the month happened after everyone had gone home. The board barely moved all day — the S&P 500 closed at 7,675.70, down 0.02%, which is a fall of a point and a half; QQQ +0.09%, SOXL +0.80%, COST −0.41%. The one that moved was Nvidia, down 1.59% to $209.66 — nerves in the hours before it had to report, while the rest of the chip shares edged higher. Then, after the close, it reported.

어제는 거의 아무 일도 없었고, 그러다 모두가 퇴근한 뒤에 이달 가장 큰 일이 일어났습니다. 표의 숫자는 하루 종일 거의 움직이지 않았습니다. S&P 500은 7,675.70으로 0.02% 내렸는데 1.5포인트가량 떨어진 것이고, QQQ +0.09%, SOXL +0.80%, COST −0.41%였습니다. 움직인 하나는 엔비디아로 1.59% 내려 209.66달러가 됐습니다. 실적을 내야 하는 몇 시간 전의 긴장이었고, 나머지 반도체 주식들은 조금씩 올랐습니다. 그리고 장이 닫힌 뒤, 발표가 나왔습니다.

The numbers beat, and not narrowly. Nvidia sold $96.2 billion of chips in three months — about $4 billion more than expected and more than double a year ago — earned $2.22 a share, and told investors to expect about $108 billion in the next three months, a figure that assumes no sales at all into China. Its shares rose about 4.7% in after-hours trading. One line went the other way, and it is the one this page has been waiting on since Monday of last week: the company expects to keep about 74 cents of profit on each sales dollar next quarter instead of 75, because the memory inside its servers costs more. So the cost worry that started this month's selling is real — it is just small so far. Watching whether it stays small is the useful thing to do from here.

숫자는 예상을 넘었고, 아슬아슬하게가 아니었습니다. 엔비디아는 석 달 동안 반도체를 962억 달러어치 팔았습니다. 예상보다 약 40억 달러 많고 1년 전의 두 배가 넘습니다. 주당 2.22달러를 벌었고, 다음 석 달은 약 1,080억 달러를 예상하라고 말했는데, 중국 쪽 매출은 전혀 없다고 가정한 숫자입니다. 주가는 시간외 거래에서 약 4.7% 올랐습니다. 반대 방향으로 간 줄이 하나 있고, 이 페이지가 지난주 월요일부터 기다려 온 것이 바로 그것입니다. 회사는 다음 분기에 매출 1달러당 남기는 이익이 75센트가 아니라 약 74센트가 될 것으로 봅니다. 서버 안의 메모리 값이 올랐기 때문입니다. 그러니 이달의 매도를 시작하게 한 비용 걱정은 실제입니다. 다만 아직은 작습니다. 그것이 계속 작게 머무는지 지켜보는 것이, 여기서부터 할 수 있는 쓸모 있는 일입니다.

Two structural things, as always. The four-way overlap is about to run upward again: one company's results are why the chip shares, the Nasdaq fund and the index are all indicated higher this morning, and the one holding with no chips in it is not. And SOXL's daily reset had its cleanest day of the month — the chip fund rose 0.26%, three times that is 0.78%, and SOXL rose 0.80%, two hundredths of a point above a perfect tripling, after coming six-tenths short on Tuesday. Do not let the good tracking distract from the arithmetic: across three days this week the chip fund is down about 0.9% and SOXL is down about 3.3% — nearly four times the fall, not three. Elsewhere, inflation came in exactly as expected and did not fall, and long-term rates ticked back up. The dated tests left: jobless claims this morning, Marvell's results after the close tonight, and the new Fed chair's first Jackson Hole speech tomorrow at 7 in the morning Pacific time.

구조적인 사실 둘은 늘 그렇듯 그대로입니다. 네 겹의 중복이 다시 위로 작동하려 하고 있습니다. 회사 하나의 실적 때문에 반도체 주식과 나스닥 펀드, 지수가 오늘 아침 모두 높게 지시되고 있고, 반도체가 들어 있지 않은 하나만 그렇지 않습니다. 그리고 SOXL의 매일 초기화는 이번 달 들어 가장 깨끗한 하루를 보냈습니다. 반도체 펀드가 0.26% 올랐으니 세 배면 0.78%인데 SOXL은 0.80% 올랐습니다. 완전한 세 배보다 0.02%포인트 위이고, 0.6%포인트가 모자랐던 화요일 뒤의 일입니다. 추적이 좋았다고 해서 셈을 놓치지는 마십시오. 이번 주 사흘 동안 반도체 펀드는 약 0.9% 내렸고 SOXL은 약 3.3% 내렸습니다. 세 배가 아니라 거의 네 배입니다. 다른 쪽에서는 물가가 예상 그대로 나왔고 내려오지 않았으며, 장기 금리는 다시 조금 올랐습니다. 남은 날짜 있는 시험대는 이렇습니다. 오늘 아침 실업수당 청구, 오늘 밤 장 마감 뒤 마벨 실적, 그리고 내일 서부 시각 오전 7시 새 연준 의장의 첫 잭슨홀 연설입니다.

Reasons it could go up

The question that hung over the market all month has now been answered in public, three times in one evening: Nvidia sold $96 billion of chips in three months and guided higher, and Salesforce and CrowdStrike beat alongside it — Salesforce had its best day since 2020. The index is back within about 0.9% of its record. The pressure that did the damage in mid-August has stayed off its worst: the 30-year rate, near 5.19%, remains below the 19-year high it touched two weeks ago, and the Treasury's bigger bond buying begins September 9. Inflation is stubborn but not accelerating — flat at 3.3% for two months. And the business economy keeps testing well: jobless claims fell to 203,000, and both big dollar-store chains beat on sales, which says shoppers are still coming through the door.

Reasons it could go down

Thursday's rise was one company wide. Most stocks in the index fell, technology was the only industry group that rose, and an index within 1% of its record while its average member falls is a market leaning very hard on a single story. The rate problem is unresolved, not solved: a 30-year government bond still pays over 5%, inflation has sat at 3.3% for two months against a 2% target, three Fed officials voted in July to raise rates — and the new chairman speaks this morning. The American household keeps sending the same message: consumer confidence at its lowest since January, and retailers marked down even on good reports — Best Buy beat, raised, and fell 4.4%. And the trade fight is now on the calendar rather than in the air: Canada taxes more than 700 American products from September 8, with a threatened 50% on cars, parts and steel behind it in January.

    Signed at the Oval Office on Thursday, before he flew to Texas for the evening, under the title “Honoring the American History of the Great Lakes and Renaming Lake Ontario as Lake America”. It is the follow-through on Tuesday's Truth Social post, further down this record, which floated the renaming “in that we don't expect to be doing much business with Ontario any longer”. What the document actually orders is narrow: the Secretary of the Interior, working with the federal Board on Geographic Names, is to rename Lake Ontario as “Lake America” within <b>30 days</b> and update the government's geographic names database. It binds federal usage only — nothing in it requires Canada, international bodies, mapmakers or anyone else to follow. Its stated reasoning is historical, with the sentence quoted above as its main economic claim, an assertion that most of the lake's volume lies in American territory, and one direct comparison: that Canada “has invested far less in similar initiatives over the same period”.
