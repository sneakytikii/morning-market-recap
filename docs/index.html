The week, in review지난 한 주 돌아보기

The week just gone was a losing one, but it did not feel like it on the last day. The market fell on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, then rose hard on Friday — the Dow up 509 points, the S&P 500 up 0.86%. Add the four days together and it still came out behind: SPX ≈ −0.80%, QQQ ≈ −0.57%, COST ≈ −1.20%, and NVDA ≈ −5.24%, the weakest of your five. The exception was SOXL ≈ +3.87%, which is the only one of the five that finished the week up. Oil was behind nearly all of it: crude climbed three days running and closed above $100 a barrel on Thursday for the first time since May, then eased on Friday, and shares went up and down with it more or less exactly.

SPX ≈ −0.80%, QQQ ≈ −0.57%, COST ≈ −1.20%, 그리고 다섯 중 가장 약했던 NVDA ≈ −5.24%입니다. 예외는 SOXL ≈ +3.87%로, 다섯 중 한 주를 상승으로 마친 유일한 종목입니다. 거의 전부가 유가가 한 일입니다. 원유는 사흘 연속 올라 목요일에 5월 이후 처음으로 배럴당 100달러를 넘겨 마쳤고, 금요일에 물러섰습니다. 주가는 그 오르내림을 거의 그대로 따라갔습니다.

The running story is what dearer oil does to everything else. Petrol at the pump averaged $4.27 a gallon, 13 cents dearer than a week earlier. The rate the American government pays to borrow for ten years rose over the week from about 4.78% to about 4.97%, and ordinary home loans crossed 7% for the first time in over a year, ending at 7.07%. Friday's August cost-of-living report was the week's main event and it came in close to expectations — prices 0.4% up on the month and 3.4% up on the year, both as forecast — which is why shares rallied. But strip out food and fuel and the rise was 0.3%, a tenth of a point more than expected, and petrol alone was more than a third of the whole monthly increase. Traders read that as settling the argument: the odds of the Federal Reserve raising rates on Wednesday, September 16 went to about 87%, from about 56% at the start of the week. Nobody can tell you what it will actually do.

Two structural things to carry into next week. The first is new: the market has started sorting technology companies by whether the artificial-intelligence boom is money coming in or money going out. Oracle beat on everything on Thursday night, opened 7.5% higher on Friday — and closed down 1.74%, because it also revealed it is spending $28.5 billion in a single quarter, about one and a half times everything it sold. The companies collecting that money went the other way on the same day: Hewlett Packard Enterprise rose 12.2% and Dell 11.5%. The second does not change. Four of your five holdings are the same bet — Nvidia outright, inside SPX, inside QQQ, and levered three times in SOXL — and SOXL remains the one that can hurt you fastest. This week shows both halves of that honestly: the four did not move as one (NVDA fell 5.24% while SOXL rose 3.87%), but on Thursday, when the chip news was genuinely bad, all four fell together and SOXL fell 8.03% in a day.

다음 주로 가져가실 구조적인 것 둘입니다. 첫째는 새로 생긴 것입니다. 시장이 기술 회사들을 인공지능 호황이 그 회사에 들어오는 돈인가 나가는 돈인가로 갈라 보기 시작했습니다. 오라클은 목요일 밤 모든 항목에서 예상을 넘겼고 금요일에 7.5% 높게 열렸다가, 1.74% 내린 채 마쳤습니다. 한 분기에 285억 달러를 쓰고 있다는 것도 함께 드러났기 때문입니다. 같은 기간 판 것 전부의 약 1.5배입니다. 그 돈을 받는 회사들은 같은 날 반대로 갔습니다. 휴렛팩커드 엔터프라이즈가 12.2%, 델이 11.5% 올랐습니다. 둘째는 달라지지 않습니다. 보유 다섯 중 넷이 같은 하나의 베팅입니다. 엔비디아를 직접, SPX 안에, QQQ 안에, 그리고 SOXL 안에 세 배로 들고 계십니다. 그리고 SOXL은 여전히 가장 빨리 다치게 할 수 있는 종목입니다. 이번 주는 그 양쪽을 정직하게 보여 줍니다. 넷이 하나처럼 움직이지는 않았지만(NVDA는 5.24% 내렸고 SOXL은 3.87% 올랐습니다), 반도체 소식이 정말로 나빴던 목요일에는 넷이 함께 내렸고 SOXL은 하루에 8.03% 내렸습니다.

Reasons it could go up

Friday showed how quickly this market rewards good news: one in-line inflation report and a small fall in oil was worth 509 points on the Dow and 0.86% on the index, breaking a four-day losing run. Underneath, the AI buildout keeps arriving as signed business rather than opinion. Oracle reported quarterly sales of $19.35 billion, up 30%, with work signed and not yet delivered of $664 billion, and Adobe beat and raised as well. The companies that collect the AI spending had an outstanding Friday — Hewlett Packard Enterprise up 12.2%, Dell up 11.5%. Earlier in the week Nvidia confirmed a $12.9 billion purchase of Hugging Face and Intel said it is raising processor prices about 10% from early October because it cannot make enough of them. The economy keeps testing well — 162,000 new jobs in August, weekly unemployment claims of 206,000 — and core inflation at 2.4% over the year is actually a touch lower than the month before. For all the noise the fear gauge ended the week at 15.84, which is a calm reading.

Reasons it could go down

The rate problem got worse this week in the way that reaches ordinary life. Long-term borrowing costs rose across all four days — the ten-year rate from about 4.78% to about 4.97%, the thirty-year from about 5.24% to about 5.36% — and ordinary home loans crossed 7% for the first time in over a year, ending at 7.07%, a 15-month high. Oil is the cause: crude above $100 a barrel on Thursday for the first time since May, petrol 13 cents dearer in a week, and Friday's figures showed petrol alone was more than a third of August's whole monthly price rise. Strip out food and fuel and prices still rose 0.3%, a tenth more than expected. The Fed votes on Wednesday, September 16 with no official speaking beforehand, and the futures market has moved to about 87% odds of a rise — a market that has spent this year pricing cuts is now pricing the opposite. Friday's rally was broad but it did not undo the week: the index still finished about 0.80% lower, and the Dow about 1.57% lower. And the four-day fall that preceded it was broad rather than narrow, with smaller American companies dropping about 1% on Thursday alone.
