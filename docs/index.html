Today · Wednesday, August 26오늘 · 8월 26일 수요일

Futures are close to flat this morning, with the technology side a touch behind — the market is sitting still ahead of two things it cannot guess. Intuit, the company behind TurboTax, reported after last night's close and is down about 11% before the open on a weaker sales forecast, pulling other software shares with it; Nvidia is up slightly. Before the open, at 5:30 Pacific time, comes the July inflation gauge the Fed watches most closely, along with the second reading of economic growth. Then Nvidia's own results after the close, about 1:20 in the afternoon Pacific, with the call at 2. Oil is lower again, which is what has been letting the bond market breathe all week — and the thing to watch is whether the chip shares hold Tuesday's bounce into that report.

오늘 아침 선물은 거의 제자리이고, 기술주 쪽이 조금 뒤처져 있습니다. 시장은 짐작할 수 없는 두 가지를 앞두고 가만히 앉아 있습니다. 터보택스를 만드는 인튜이트는 어젯밤 장 마감 뒤 실적을 냈는데, 매출 전망을 낮춰 잡아 개장 전 약 11% 내려 있고 다른 소프트웨어 주식들도 함께 끌어내리고 있습니다. 엔비디아는 조금 올라 있습니다. 장이 열리기 전 서부 시각 오전 5시 30분에 연준이 가장 중시하는 7월 물가 지표가 나오고, 경제 성장률 2차 발표도 함께 나옵니다. 그다음은 엔비디아 자체 실적으로 장 마감 뒤, 서부 시각 오후 1시 20분쯤이며 설명회는 2시입니다. 유가는 오늘도 내렸는데, 이번 주 내내 채권 시장이 숨을 돌린 이유가 그것입니다. 지켜볼 것은 반도체 주식이 화요일의 반등을 그 발표까지 지켜내는지입니다.

Tuesday, in review화요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday the market took Monday back. The chip shares that had been sold hardest were bought again — NVDA +2.19%, SOXL +4.06%, QQQ +0.62% — the S&P 500 rose 0.32% to 7,677.28, and the Dow added 160 points for a third gain in a row. Nvidia's seven-day losing run ended the day before it has to report, and it reports today, after the close. The one that went the other way was Costco, down 1.17% to $960.01, giving back about half of Monday's jump.

어제 시장은 월요일을 되돌렸습니다. 가장 세게 팔렸던 반도체 주식들이 다시 사졌습니다. NVDA +2.19%, SOXL +4.06%, QQQ +0.62%였고, S&P 500은 0.32% 올라 7,677.28이 됐으며, 다우존스는 160포인트 올라 사흘 연속 상승했습니다. 엔비디아의 일곱 거래일 연속 하락은 실적을 발표해야 하는 날 하루 전에 끝났고, 발표는 오늘 장 마감 뒤입니다. 반대로 간 하나는 코스트코로 1.17% 내려 960.01달러가 됐고, 월요일 상승분의 절반쯤을 돌려줬습니다.

Nothing was actually resolved. The worry that started Monday's selling — reports that servers built around Nvidia's chips will cost more than 15% more from early next year, because the memory inside them has got so expensive — is exactly where it was; the market simply paid the price back while it waits. Meanwhile the news about American households got worse in three separate places: consumer confidence fell to its lowest since January, new home sales to their weakest since January, and Dick's Sporting Goods lost about 30% in one session after cutting its forecast for the year. With Walmart last Thursday, that is three warnings about the shopper in six days.

실제로 정리된 것은 없습니다. 월요일의 매도를 시작하게 한 걱정, 곧 엔비디아 반도체를 넣은 서버 값이 그 안의 메모리가 너무 비싸져 내년 초부터 15% 넘게 오른다는 보도는 그대로입니다. 시장은 기다리는 동안 값만 도로 치렀을 뿐입니다. 한편 미국 가계에 관한 소식은 서로 다른 세 곳에서 더 나빠졌습니다. 소비자 신뢰지수는 1월 이후 최저였고, 신규 주택 판매도 1월 이후 최저였으며, 딕스 스포팅 굿즈는 연간 전망을 낮춘 뒤 한 거래일에 약 30%를 잃었습니다. 지난 목요일 월마트까지 더하면 엿새 사이 소비자에 관한 경고가 셋입니다.

Two structural things showed up plainly, and this time in the pleasant direction. The first is the four-way overlap this page always flags, running upward: one chip rebound produced +2.19%, +4.06%, +0.62% and +0.32% on the same afternoon, in that order of size, while the one holding with no chips in it went the other way. The second is SOXL's daily reset, and it is worth reading carefully this morning: the chip fund rose 1.56%, three times that is 4.67%, and SOXL rose 4.06% — most of the tripling but about six-tenths of a point short, the widest gap of the month. Over the two days it is down about 4.1% while the chip fund is down about 1.2%. Elsewhere, long-term rates eased a second day and oil fell again. The dated tests: the inflation report before the open and Nvidia's results after the close, both today, and the new Fed chair's first Jackson Hole speech on Friday at 7 in the morning Pacific time.

구조적인 사실 둘이 분명하게 드러났는데, 이번에는 기분 좋은 방향이었습니다. 첫째는 이 페이지가 늘 짚는 네 겹의 중복이 위로 작동한 것입니다. 반도체 반등 하나가 같은 오후에 +2.19%, +4.06%, +0.62%, +0.32%를 만들었고 크기의 순서까지 그대로였으며, 반도체가 들어 있지 않은 하나만 반대로 갔습니다. 둘째는 SOXL의 매일 초기화이고, 오늘 아침에는 이것을 특히 꼼꼼히 읽어 두실 만합니다. 반도체 펀드가 1.56% 올랐으니 세 배면 4.67%인데 SOXL은 4.06% 올랐습니다. 세 배의 대부분이지만 약 0.6%포인트가 모자랐고, 이번 달 들어 가장 큰 차이입니다. 이틀을 합치면 이 펀드는 약 4.1% 하락, 반도체 펀드는 약 1.2% 하락입니다. 다른 쪽에서는 장기 금리가 이틀째 내렸고 유가도 또 내렸습니다. 날짜 있는 시험대는 이렇습니다. 오늘, 개장 전 물가 보고서와 장 마감 뒤 엔비디아 실적, 그리고 금요일 서부 시각 오전 7시 새 연준 의장의 첫 잭슨홀 연설입니다.

Reasons it could go up

The market keeps refusing to break. Monday's chip scare lasted exactly one session: AMD, Nvidia and Micron were all bought back on Tuesday, and the index is now within about 1.6% of its record. The pressure that did the damage all last month has eased two days running — the 10-year rate is down to about 4.63% and the 30-year to about 5.17%, off its 19-year high — and the Treasury's bigger bond buying only begins September 9. Oil has fallen about 2% on each of the last two days, which takes pressure off the inflation that rates are built from. And the business economy underneath keeps testing well: Friday's survey of American firms was the strongest since April 2022, and jobless claims fell to 206,000.

Reasons it could go down

The households are where the news keeps getting worse. Consumer confidence fell to 89.4 on Tuesday, its lowest since January; new home sales slowed to their weakest since January; and Dick's Sporting Goods was marked down about 30% in one session for cutting its forecast, blaming weaker demand for trainers and clothing and heavier discounting. Walmart said the same thing last Thursday. A 30-year government bond still pays over 5%, which competes with every expensive share in this index, and the two days of relief came from news reports rather than any decision. The index's biggest company reports today into a question Monday raised and nothing has answered: servers built around its chips reportedly cost more than 15% more from early next year. And the trade fight with Canada is now scheduled rather than threatened — more than 700 American products taxed from September 8, on top of a threat to raise tariffs on all Canadian cars, trucks, parts and steel to 50% in January.
