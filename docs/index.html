Today · Wednesday, October 7오늘 · 10월 7일 수요일

The morning is leaning against yesterday's records. Share futures point modestly lower — the Nasdaq side most — as the ten-year borrowing rate climbs back near 5.33%, above Monday's record close, and oil sits just under $90. Chip shares are the soft spot: memory maker Micron is down about 3% before the open and the chip index nearly 2% — three times that for SOXL, indicated down about 5% — after a well-known bearish investor was reported extending bets against Nvidia and Micron into next year. Costco is slightly higher ahead of its big evening. The crowded day stands: a large auction of ten-year bonds at ten in the morning Pacific time, the notes from the Fed's September meeting at 11, and Costco's September sales after the close tonight. The question underneath is unchanged — whether record-priced shares keep shrugging at a record-priced bond.

오늘 아침은 어제의 기록들과 반대로 기울어 있습니다. 주가지수 선물은 소폭 낮게 — 나스닥 쪽이 가장 — 가리키고 있습니다. 10년물 차입 금리가 월요일 기록 종가보다 높은 약 5.33%로 되올라왔고, 유가는 90달러 바로 아래에 있습니다. 약한 곳은 반도체입니다. 하락에 거는 것으로 유명한 투자자가 엔비디아와 마이크론에 대한 베팅을 내년까지 연장했다는 보도 뒤, 메모리 회사 마이크론이 개장 전 약 3%, 반도체 지수가 2% 가까이 내려 있습니다 — SOXL에는 그 세 배가 적용돼 약 5% 하락을 가리킵니다. 코스트코는 자신의 큰 저녁을 앞두고 소폭 올라 있습니다. 붐비는 하루는 그대로입니다. 서부 시각 오전 10시 대규모 10년물 국채 입찰, 11시 연준 9월 회의 기록, 그리고 장 마감 뒤 코스트코 9월 매출. 밑에 깔린 질문은 그대로입니다 — 기록적인 값의 주식이 기록적인 이자를 주는 국채 앞에서 계속 어깨를 으쓱할 수 있는가.

Tuesday, in review화요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday the S&P 500 finally closed at a record — its first since August 13, and its first close ever above 7,800. It rose 0.58% to 7,818.93. The Nasdaq added 0.45% for a second straight record close, and the Nasdaq-100 fund QQQ made it three in a row. Nvidia squeezed out a second straight record of its own — by 34 cents, after touching a new high of about $243 in the morning and fading all day. What opened the door was the other side of the seesaw giving ground at last: the ten-year borrowing rate eased to about 5.27% from Monday's highest-since-2007 record, and oil spent a second day settled below $90.

Underneath, the same narrow machine — with a rotating driver. The record was carried by the AI giants again, but yesterday's chip cheer belonged to AMD, up 2.8% after analysts raised their targets sharply and its chief promised substantially more chip supply next year, while Nvidia faded and plain chip shares finished flat for a second day — Intel fell 3.2%, memory maker Micron 1.7%. Smaller companies fell outright. One published count says about 70% of the index's own members still sit at least 10% below their recent highs — a record carried by few hands. Costco was the day's quiet winner, up 1.32% to its best close since September 1, the evening before its own sales report.

그 밑은 똑같이 좁은 기계입니다 — 운전자만 바뀌었습니다. 기록을 나른 것은 이번에도 AI 거인들이었지만, 어제 반도체의 환호는 AMD의 몫이었습니다. 애널리스트들의 목표가 상향과 내년 공급을 크게 늘리겠다는 약속에 2.8% 올랐습니다. 그동안 엔비디아는 밀렸고, 일반 반도체 주식은 이틀째 제자리였습니다 — 인텔은 3.2%, 메모리 회사 마이크론은 1.7% 내렸습니다. 작은 회사들은 아예 내렸습니다. 한 공표된 집계는 지수 구성 종목의 약 70%가 여전히 자신의 최근 고점보다 10% 이상 아래라고 셉니다 — 몇 안 되는 손이 나르는 기록입니다. 코스트코는 이날의 조용한 승자였습니다. 1.32% 올라 9월 1일 이후 가장 높은 종가, 자신의 매출 발표 전날 저녁이었습니다.

Today is the crowded day the calendar has been pointing at all week. At ten in the morning Pacific time the government auctions ten-year bonds at the dearest rates in a generation; at 11 the notes from the Fed's September meeting come out; and after the close tonight Costco reports September sales. This morning the seesaw has already tilted back — the ten-year rate is near 5.33% again, and chip shares are sharply lower before the open, which lands three times as hard on SOXL. The standing warnings hold: four of your five are the same chip bet, and SOXL triples whatever chips do each day — these past five days that paid +11.7%; in a flat July it cost 57%. Nobody can tell you which way today breaks.

Reasons it could go up

The record is back — earned through a rate rise, an oil spike and a chip scare, with the year's gain now about 14.2%. The two numbers the Fed watches most both came in friendly within the past two weeks: inflation cooled to a 3.0% core pace, and September's 29,000 new jobs were weak enough to take an October rate rise mostly off the table — market odds sit at about one in five. The chip side keeps publishing receipts — Micron's sold-out forecast, AMD promising substantially more supply, a second straight Nvidia record close — and oil has now settled below $90 two days running, a quiet subtraction from every delivered price.

Reasons it could go down
