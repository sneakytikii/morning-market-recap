Reasons it could go up

The index just took the season's two biggest tests back to back — a Fed warning and the first giant AI report cards — and sits within about 1.0% of its record. The Fed's own reading of the economy was an upgrade: growing at a strong pace, with business investment rising fast. The earnings keep beating — Microsoft grew its cloud business 48% and says demand still exceeds supply — and the memory makers' own forecast has chip demand outrunning supply for years. Most important, the market showed on Wednesday night that it can reward as well as punish: the giant that spent less than forecast was paid about 4% for it. That is how a repricing ends — not with the spending stopping, but with the spending being graded, bill by bill. The year's gain sits near 13%, and October, which looked lost two weeks ago, has firmed to roughly +1.2%.

Reasons it could go down
