Today · Wednesday, August 12오늘 · 8월 12일 수요일

The market opened higher this morning and has been handing some of it back. The July inflation report came out before the bell: prices rose 0.1% over the month and 3.4% over the year, a shade slower than June and about what was expected, with petrol down 2.9%. That was the biggest scheduled number of the month and it passed without trouble — traders have tilted toward the Federal Reserve leaving interest rates alone in September. Chip shares took it best, helped by CoreWeave, whose sales more than doubled in results published after last night's close: SOXL opened about 11% higher and was holding roughly 8% by mid-morning Pacific time, and Nvidia is up a couple of percent. The broader index has drifted back toward where it started the day. Cisco reports after the close tonight, and Applied Materials — the cleaner read on chips — tomorrow.

오늘 장은 높게 출발했다가 오른 폭을 조금씩 되돌리고 있습니다. 7월 물가 보고서가 개장 전에 나왔습니다. 물가는 한 달 사이 0.1%, 1년 사이 3.4% 올라 6월보다 조금 느렸고 대체로 예상과 같았으며, 휘발유값은 2.9% 내렸습니다. 이번 달 예정된 지표 중 가장 큰 것이었는데 별 탈 없이 지나갔고, 시장의 무게는 '연방준비제도가 9월에는 금리를 그대로 둘 것'이라는 쪽으로 기울었습니다. 반도체 주식이 가장 잘 받아들였습니다. 어젯밤 장 마감 후 나온 실적에서 매출이 두 배 넘게 늘어난 코어위브가 힘을 보탰습니다. SOXL은 약 11% 높게 출발해 서부 시각 오전 늦은 시간 기준 8% 안팎을 지키고 있고, 엔비디아도 2% 남짓 올라 있습니다. 지수 전체는 출발점 부근으로 되밀려 왔습니다. 오늘 밤 장 마감 후에는 시스코가, 내일은 반도체를 더 깨끗하게 보여 주는 어플라이드 머티어리얼즈가 실적을 발표합니다.

Tuesday, in review화요일 장 돌아보기

Yesterday the market went down, and for once the chips had nothing to do with it. The S&P 500 fell 0.32% to 7,728.20, the Nasdaq 0.60% and the Dow 0.34% — while chip shares actually rose about 0.9%. So your five split the opposite way to Monday: SOXL was the only gainer, up 2.31%, NVDA finished all but unchanged at −0.02%, QQQ fell 0.34%, and Costco was the weakest of the lot at −0.88%.

어제 시장은 내렸는데, 이번만큼은 반도체와 상관이 없었습니다. S&P 500은 0.32% 내린 7,728.20, 나스닥은 0.60%, 다우는 0.34% 내렸습니다. 그 사이 반도체 주식은 오히려 약 0.9% 올랐습니다. 그래서 보유하신 다섯은 월요일과 정반대로 갈렸습니다. SOXL이 2.31% 올라 유일한 상승 종목이었고, NVDA는 −0.02%로 사실상 제자리, QQQ는 0.34% 내렸으며, 코스트코가 −0.88%로 가장 약했습니다.

What fell instead was the rest of big technology, and the reason is worth understanding because it is new. Alphabet, the owner of Google, dropped about 3.8% — not because it is earning less, but because of what it is spending. It told investors it will put up to $205 billion into artificial-intelligence equipment this year, roughly double last year, and that it had borrowed $25 billion to help pay for it. For the first time, more cash is leaving that business than coming in. That is the same question that knocked Nvidia down 2.86% on Monday, asked from the other end: Nvidia is arranging half a trillion dollars so its customers can buy the machines, and Alphabet is one of the customers now borrowing to do it. Neither fact says the money is wasted. Both say the market has started pricing the bill rather than the promise. Underneath all of it, the standoff with Iran hardened again — the President rejected Iran's demand for war compensation and Iran's foreign minister said talks could not restart — though long-term borrowing rates still eased for a second day, the 10-year from 4.71% to 4.68%.

대신 내린 것은 나머지 큰 기술 기업들이었고, 그 이유는 새로운 것이라 알아 두실 만합니다. 구글을 가진 알파벳이 약 3.8% 떨어졌는데, 덜 벌어서가 아니라 쓰는 돈 때문이었습니다. 올해 인공지능 장비에 최대 2,050억 달러를 넣겠다고 밝혔는데 작년의 두 배 가까운 금액이고, 그 자금을 대려고 250억 달러를 빌렸다고 했습니다. 이 사업에서 들어오는 현금보다 나가는 현금이 많아진 것이 처음입니다. 월요일에 엔비디아를 2.86% 끌어내린 것과 같은 질문을 반대편에서 던진 셈입니다. 엔비디아는 고객이 장비를 살 수 있도록 5,000억 달러를 마련하고 있고, 알파벳은 이제 돈을 빌려서 사는 고객 중 하나입니다. 두 사실 어느 쪽도 그 돈이 헛되다는 뜻은 아닙니다. 다만 시장이 약속이 아니라 청구서에 값을 매기기 시작했다는 뜻입니다. 그 아래에서는 이란과의 대치가 다시 굳어졌습니다. 대통령은 이란의 전쟁 배상 요구를 거부했고 이란 외무장관은 협상을 다시 시작할 수 없다고 말했습니다. 그래도 장기 금리는 이틀째 내려 10년물이 4.71%에서 4.68%가 됐습니다.

The structural point is still the structural point, but yesterday showed it from an angle worth keeping. One story — chips — normally moves four of your five together, and Monday was that: chips down about 3%, four holdings down, and Costco, the one that owns no chips, the only gainer. Yesterday ran the same machinery backwards: chips up, SOXL the only gainer, Costco the only real loser. Two days, opposite directions, one explanation. The cost of it is now visible — Costco is the only one of your five that is down for August. After yesterday's close, one of Nvidia's biggest customers, CoreWeave, reported sales more than double a year earlier and $104.2 billion of work already booked; its shares rose about 16%. That is real money being spent on these machines, though Nvidia part-owns the company reporting it. The independent read comes tomorrow, Thursday August 13, when Applied Materials reports, and the one that settles it is Nvidia's own results on Wednesday, August 26.

구조적인 이야기는 여전히 그대로지만, 어제는 그것을 기억해 둘 만한 각도에서 보여 줬습니다. 반도체라는 이야기 하나가 보통 보유 5개 중 넷을 함께 움직이는데, 월요일이 바로 그랬습니다. 반도체가 약 3% 내리자 네 종목이 함께 내렸고, 반도체가 없는 코스트코만 올랐습니다. 어제는 같은 장치가 거꾸로 돌았습니다. 반도체가 오르자 SOXL만 올랐고, 사실상 코스트코만 내렸습니다. 이틀, 반대 방향, 설명은 하나입니다. 그 대가는 이제 눈에 보입니다. 코스트코는 보유 5개 중 8월에 유일하게 내린 종목입니다. 어제 장이 끝난 뒤에는 엔비디아의 가장 큰 고객 중 하나인 코어위브가 1년 전의 두 배가 넘는 매출과 이미 확보한 일감 1,042억 달러를 발표했고, 주가는 약 16% 올랐습니다. 이 장비에 실제 돈이 쓰이고 있다는 뜻입니다. 다만 그 회사의 지분을 엔비디아가 갖고 있습니다. 독립적인 확인은 내일, 8월 13일 목요일 어플라이드 머티어리얼즈 실적에서 나오고, 최종적으로 판가름 나는 자리는 8월 26일 수요일 엔비디아 실적입니다.

Reasons it could go up

Company profits are coming in strong and the big technology firms have committed to enormous spending. This morning's inflation report came in about as expected rather than hot, which removes the single biggest scheduled threat of the month. Tuesday's fall was small and narrow — one group, communication companies, did most of it, while chip shares actually rose — and the index is still within half a percent of a record. The fear gauge closed at 15.28, which means investors are relaxed rather than braced.

Reasons it could go down

The index is about half a percent from a record, which means a lot of good news is already in the price. Long-term government bonds still pay about 5.24% with far less risk, and when safe returns are that good, shares look less attractive by comparison. Tuesday showed a newer worry: the market is starting to ask what the enormous spending on artificial intelligence actually earns. Alphabet fell about 3.8% for saying it will spend up to $205 billion this year and borrow $25 billion to help pay for it — the same question that knocked Nvidia down 2.86% on Monday. And the oil story has no date on it. One piece of news out of the Middle East is still enough to move this whole market in a session.

What I'd watch: the big one is behind us. The July inflation report came out this morning before the open, and it was kind: prices rose 0.1% over the month and 3.4% over the year, a shade slower than June's 3.5% and about what was expected. Petrol prices fell 2.9% in the month. That matters because Friday's record was built on the belief that the Federal Reserve has finished raising interest rates — it has not cut them, the rate has sat at 3.5% to 3.75% for five meetings, and three officials wanted it higher at the last one. A hot reading this morning would have taken that belief away. It did not, and traders have tilted from an even split toward the Fed leaving rates alone in September.
